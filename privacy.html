Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains how we handle your information on YoSoko.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account details: Name, email, phone when you register
  • Listings: What you post for sale or want to buy
  • Activity: How you use the platform

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To connect buyers and sellers on campus
  • To improve your experience on YoSoko
  • To keep the marketplace safe and functional

3. Data Sharing & Security

We never sell your data. Contact info is only shared between users when necessary for transactions.

We use Firebase (by Google) for secure data storage and authentication. While we implement strong security measures, please be cautious about what personal info you share.

4. Your Rights & Contact

You can update your profile info anytime. To delete your account, email us at yosoko25@gmail.

Questions? Reach our support team at the same email. We're happy to help!
